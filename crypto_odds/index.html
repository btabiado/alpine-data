Options-market research · BTC · ETH · XRP

Where do option traders think Bitcoin, Ether and XRP will settle on each listed expiry date? This page reads the answer out of live Deribit option prices, then asks a second question: can price history or machine learning predict these moves better than a simple base rate?

Market-implied, not a forecastProbabilities come from option prices, which include a risk premium (traders pay up for protection). Read them as the market's pricing, not a prediction or advice.
Ranges show quote uncertaintyOptions trade with a bid and an ask. The quote range is every probability consistent with those prices: wide when the market is thin, tight when it is liquid.
“Not quoted” is not 0%Far from today's price no options are quoted, so the market says nothing there. Those targets are marked rather than guessed.
